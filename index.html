02 / ABOUT

Built to explore underwater vision.

The TrashCan dataset contains annotated frames from remotely operated vehicles exploring the seafloor. Submerged investigates how transfer learning and image augmentation affect the detection of trash and marine life.

This demonstration uses the Instance labeling scheme (22 object categories). Bounding-box predictions may be inaccurate, especially for rare classes or low-visibility scenes. The site is a student research demonstration, not an operational monitoring system.

Project team: Steven Johnson, Carter Latham, Brian Rodriguez, and Jacob Scheibe · Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology.
